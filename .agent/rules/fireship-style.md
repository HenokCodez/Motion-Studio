# VantaBrief Production & Style Directive (Supercedes Fireship Legacy)

All production in this workspace is governed by the authoritative **[VantaBrief Visual Style Bible](file:///D:/Automations/motion-studio/Channels/VantaBrief/style/VantaBrief_Visual_Style_Bible.md)**.

## Core Operational Directives
1. **Rule R1**: Strictly NO sound effects, NO background music, NO audio ducking, NO added audio of any kind. Untouched narration audio (`audio.mp3`) is the single audio source.
2. **Rule R2**: Untouched voiceover. Visuals adapt to voiceover timestamps; never trim, speed up, or re-time the voiceover.
3. **Rule R3**: Palette strictly from `tokens.ts` (Charcoal `#231F20`, Cream `#FAF4D9`, Electric blue `#1677FF`, Amber `#F59E0B`, Crimson `#EF4444`, Emerald `#10B981`).
4. **Rule R4**: Strict character ban. No human characters, stick figures, cartoon people, avatars, sloths, or mascots. Default: NO characters.
5. **Rule R5**: Standardized `FOOTAGE NEEDED` placeholders for any unsupplied footage or demonstrations.
6. **Rule R6**: Clean motion graphics, NO bottom full-sentence subtitles. Screen focus on kinetic key phrases (1–4 words) and clean technical data.
7. **5-Style System**: Kinetic typography (1), Real evidence (2), Editorial collage (3), Graphic symbols (4), Technical motion graphics (5 - reserved for explanations).
