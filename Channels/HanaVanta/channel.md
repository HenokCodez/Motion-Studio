# Channel Identity Guide: HanaVanta

## Creative Overview
- **Channel ID**: `HanaVanta`
- **Core Purpose**: In-depth cinematic documentaries, historical retrospectives, technological evolutions, and investigative case studies.
- **Tone**: Contemplative, immersive, narrative-driven, visually rich and deliberate.

## Visual Identity
- **Canvas**: 16:9 Landscape (1920x1080) with cinematic 2.39:1 crop option for dramatic sequences.
- **Backgrounds**: Warm dark charcoal (`#0C0D10`) with subtle amber and sepia atmospheric vignettes.
- **Accents**: Warm Golden Amber (`#F59E0B`), Terracotta Crimson (`#E11D48`), and Muted Teal (`#14B8A6`).
- **Cards & Overlays**: Editorial panels (`rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.80)`), warm sandstone border lines, generous whitespace, serif headings.

## Motion Principles
- **Spring Physics**: Gentle, cinematic, organic easing without excessive bounce (`damping: 20`, `mass: 1.0`, `stiffness: 85`).
- **Pacing**: Deliberate, allowing viewers to absorb detailed maps, documents, and historical photography.
- **Captions**: Classic documentary subtitle style with warm highlights.

## Common Scene Motifs
1. **Documentary Maps**: Vintage / warm cartography with animated route lines, trade corridors, and territorial boundaries.
2. **Archival / Image Treatments**: Smooth Ken Burns pan & zoom with subtle grain overlay.
3. **Timeline Scrollers**: Horizontal historical timeline with dates and marker highlights.
4. **Footage Placeholders**: Clearly labeled studio placeholders for host camera segments, physical archival inspections, or location b-roll.
